# /clip:content.screenshot

Capture a screenshot of a URL and copy it to the clipboard.

## Usage

`/clip:content.screenshot <url>`

## Steps

1. Capture a screenshot of the page (prefer full-page unless it produces an unusably tall image).
2. Copy the screenshot to clipboard.

## Output

Respond with a short confirmation only.
